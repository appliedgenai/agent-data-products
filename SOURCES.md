# Research and source boundaries

Companion to [Data Products for the Agent Economy](README.md). Primary sources reviewed 28 September 2026; unchanged technical evidence was reused from the earlier research, and agent-consumption mechanisms were checked for this revision.

The product catalog, response envelope, synthetic consumption record, diagrams, acceptance rules and adoption model are the author's proposals. The cited capabilities do not establish that this combined architecture has been deployed or benchmarked. Gartner and McKinsey references are public guidance, not paid research or endorsements of this design. Platinum is optional terminology proposed here.

## Evidence used in the paper

## Adoption and evidence limits

Recheck the product version, plan, region, execution identity and effective serving permissions before deployment. Evaluate candidate engines against the same product contract and representative consumer tasks. No universal platform combination, GraphRAG improvement or financial return is claimed.

The numerical example uses synthetic frozen order inputs. Its 17 checks demonstrate selected metric arithmetic and contract failures; they do not implement the graph, retrieval service, protocol adapters, authentication, product composition or the paper's proposed consumption record. Historical replay, correction-state preservation and full source completeness require separate implementation and evaluation. Fixed-offset time examples do not test daylight-saving behavior.

The paper's professional background reflects supplied author information. Its scenario and proposed architecture are independent work, not reported employer deployments or measured outcomes.
